About the project

Starring Innovative Treatments for Component Healing with New Grade-aluminium alloy

A metal that closes its own pores

Self-healing is well established in polymers but far harder in metals. ALMAZIUM®, developed at UCLouvain, is an Al-Mg-Zr alloy whose healing phase melts during a heat treatment, flows into micro-pores and cracks, and seals them.

The alloy builds on the ERC project ALUFIX and the M-ERA.NET project HAMAAC, and was designed for laser powder bed fusion (PBF-LB). STITCHING extends it to wire-based processes, DED-arc and DED-laser, which can build much larger parts at lower economic and environmental cost than powder. The project also studies how the alloy can be welded, both to itself and to conventional wrought aluminium, and observes the healing mechanisms directly as they happen.

Target applications are lightweight structures for aerospace and defence, and thermal management systems such as heat exchangers for space. Healing also supports a "recycling by repairing" approach, where damaged or worn components are restored instead of replaced.

Strength and ductility together

High-strength aluminium printed by PBF-LB usually trades ductility for strength. ALMAZIUM® keeps both, and its strength stays stable at elevated temperature, including after the healing treatment.

Healing at 400–550 °C. A heat treatment in this range recovers micro-defects up to 1 µm and heals porosities of 2–3 µm, whereas pores in conventional PBF-LB Al-Si alloys generally grow on heating.

Objectives

  • Manufacture with wire

    Demonstrate that ALMAZIUM® can be produced by DED-arc and DED-laser, enabling large parts while cutting the economic and environmental costs of powder processing.

  • Weld reliably

    Evaluate TIG welding of ALMAZIUM® to itself and to conventional wrought aluminium alloys, using its thermal stability and resistance to porosity.

  • See healing in-situ

    Develop multi-scale in-situ characterisation, including tensile testing in the SEM and X-ray tomography up to 550 °C, to understand and optimise damage and healing.

  • Assess the life cycle

    Quantify the environmental and economic performance of powder- and wire-based routes to select the most resource- and cost-efficient pathway.

  • Prove industrial relevance

    Integrate ALMAZIUM® into demonstrator components for aerospace, defence and thermal management systems.
