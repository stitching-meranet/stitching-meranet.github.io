Partners

Eight organisations from Belgium, Czechia and France, combining alloy design, additive manufacturing, welding, characterisation and industrial application.

Belgium

UCLouvainCoordinator

Université catholique de Louvain

Developer of ALMAZIUM®. Coordinates the project and leads the additive manufacturing work (WP1) and the environmental and economic assessment (WP6).

Website

AnyShape

Industrial metal additive manufacturing (PBF-LB) for aerospace, defence and high-performance applications. Produces samples and demonstrators.

Website

Euro Heat Pipes (EHP)

Thermal products for space. Leads the TIG welding work (WP2) and provides thermal management demonstrators.

Website

Belgian Welding Institute (BWI)

Welding and DED-arc additive manufacturing. Leads dissemination, communication and exploitation (WP7).

Website

Czechia

CEITEC BUT

Central European Institute of Technology, Brno University of Technology

Laboratory X-ray computed tomography. Leads the standard characterisation at micro to macro scale (WP3).

Website

CactuX

CactuX

Develops the high-temperature in-situ heating stage for X-ray tomography and leads WP4.

Website

France

ONERA

The French Aerospace Lab

In-situ mechanical testing in the scanning electron microscope and modelling. Leads the multiscale in-situ characterisation (WP5).

Website

Institut Maupertuis

DED-laser additive manufacturing of ALMAZIUM® samples and demonstrators.
