The STITCHING project is officially underway

The consortium gathered for the kick-off meeting in Louvain-la-Neuve, Belgium, hosted by UCLouvain, with partners joining both in person and online. The meeting marks the start of three years of collaborative research on the self-healing ALMAZIUM® aluminium alloy for wire-based additive manufacturing and welding.

The meeting brought together the nine partners from Belgium, France and Czechia:

Belgium
UCLouvain, AnyShape, Euro Heat Pipes (EHP), Belgian Welding Institute (BWI)
France
ONERA, Institut Maupertuis, Mines Saint-Étienne
Czechia
CEITEC, CactuX
